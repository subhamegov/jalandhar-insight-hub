# MoHUA Urban Intelligence: prototype explainer

MoHUA Urban Intelligence is a decision-support prototype for senior ministry officials to understand urban conditions and delivery across missions, states and cities. It brings together the data fields currently used across MoHUA mission records to connect investments, infrastructure, services and citizen outcomes. Officials can begin with a geography or a decision signal, open the underlying records and identify where administrative attention or cross-mission coordination may be needed.

## How to use it

Choose National or City scope in the header, then start from the map or an observed condition. Open a Decision Signal, an urban system or a delivery record, and follow the linked projects, assets, investments, agencies and evidence. Use the connected records to review a decision or prepare a briefing.

## Sample navigation flows

**1. Understanding a city.** City Overview, City Map, Localities or Projects, linked assets and services, Evidence. Shows what government is delivering in a place and what remains unresolved.

**2. Identifying a cross-mission decision.** National View, Decision Signals, connected missions and projects, Planning Interventions, Executive Briefing. Shows where a cross-mission review or coordination exercise may be needed.

**3. Housing service readiness.** Housing, its water and sewerage dependencies, related Projects and Investment, then Outcomes and Evidence. Shows whether completed housing is supported by essential services, without treating project completion as proof of occupancy or service reliability.

## Data note

This demonstration combines Jalandhar's evidence-backed records with synthetic prototype observations for Thane, Surat, Ahmedabad, Guwahati and Karnal. Synthetic figures are illustrative and must not be read as official statistics or live mission performance.
