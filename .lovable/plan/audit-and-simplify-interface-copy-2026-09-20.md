# Audit and simplify interface copy

## Scope
- Review all visible text across pages, navigation, cards, tables, maps, dialogs, notices, metadata, empty states, and tooltips.
- Remove em dashes and semicolons from interface copy, replacing them with full stops, commas, colons, parentheses, or separate labels as appropriate.
- Shorten dense, repetitive, or formulaic copy so users can scan it quickly.

## Approach
- Preserve all facts, caveats, classifications, IDs, units, dates, calculations, routes, and source data.
- Keep essential prototype and data-quality warnings, but express them in short, direct sentences.
- Prefer concise headings, one-sentence descriptions, and fact-first labels.
- Leave programming syntax, generated route files, raw supplied datasets, and internal comments unchanged unless they appear in the interface.

## Validation
- Re-scan authored interface files to confirm no visible em dashes or semicolons remain.
- Check representative National and City pages at desktop and narrow widths for wrapping or overflow.
- Verify the app builds cleanly and that navigation and interactions are unchanged.
